\section*{Research Overview}
I am a senior undergraduate in Computer Engineering in the UIUC--Zhejiang University dual-degree program. \textbf{I am seeking Ph.D. opportunities in machine learning systems (MLSys) starting in Fall 2027.}

My research focuses on agentic LLM serving, hardware-aware sequence modeling, and efficient long-context multimodal systems. My recent work includes \link{https://inference-rsi.com/}{Evolving Infra} (co-first author), on agent-adaptive serving, and~\link{https://flashstates.ai/}{FlashStates} (co-first author and project lead), on hybrid state management.
